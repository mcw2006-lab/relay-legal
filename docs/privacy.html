Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Data Relay handles

  • Voice transcripts and typed commands, AI replies, spoken summaries, and conversation links.
  • Provider-tab readiness, timestamps, your assistant preferences, and pairing settings.
  • Encrypted SQLite prompt history on your Mac (currently stored in ~/.jarvis-history.db) and provider API credentials in macOS Keychain when you enable an API route.
  • Optional diagnostic exports contain connection and readiness information. Relay does not currently send automatic crash or selector reports.

How data moves

The browser extension reads and enters chat text on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to carry out your commands. It sends replies to your local Relay bridge. Relay does not collect browser passwords, export session cookies, or operate a central prompt or history database.

Dictation may be processed by iOS, Safari, or your browser’s speech recognition service. Processing location depends on your device and settings. If you use a Cloudflare Tunnel, Cloudflare transports requests between your phone and Mac over HTTPS. Your selected AI provider, including an alternate provider if you enable fallback, receives the commands you ask Relay to run. An optional custom API endpoint receives the prompts you send to it. Those services handle data under their own policies.

  • Apple and Cloudflare
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Relay’s use of information received from the Chrome extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Relay uses this data to deliver and support the voice bridge. Relay does not sell it, use it for advertising, or allow its staff to read your prompts through the product.

Retention and control

Use Settings → Wipe prompt history to clear Relay’s local prompt and reply history or choose a retention period. Your AI providers store their own conversations separately. Disable or uninstall Relay’s extension in Chrome or Safari to revoke its access to those tabs. To uninstall, quit Relay and move Relay.app to the Trash, then remove the extension in your browser’s extension settings. Uninstalling does not erase local files or Keychain entries. To remove those, use the history wipe first, then remove the Relay configuration and pairing files and Relay entries in macOS Keychain Access. This version has no Relay cloud accounts to delete. Contact your AI providers about accounts you use with them.
